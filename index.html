Master Final Stable Syllables (`Consonant + le`)

The "Count Back 3" Rule: When a word ends with a Consonant + le (like -ble, -ple, -dle, -fle, -tle, -gle, -cle), count back 3 letters from the end and slice off that final stable syllable first!
